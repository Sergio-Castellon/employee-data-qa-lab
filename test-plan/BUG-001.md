
# BUG-001 — Employee Registration Rejects Valid Age 65

## Bug Information

**Bug ID:** BUG-001

**Title:** Employee Registration — Valid age 65 incorrectly rejected

**Component:** Employee Registration

**Related Test Case:** TC-003 — Maximum Valid Age

**Severity:** Medium (Proposed)

**Priority:** Medium (Proposed)

**Status:** Simulated — Not reproduced against a working application

**Environment:** Employee Data QA Lab — Hypothetical registration application

---

## Bug Description

The employee registration application incorrectly rejects employees who are exactly 65 years old.

According to requirement REQ-003, employees must be between 18 and 65 years old, inclusive.

Since age 65 falls within the valid range, registration should succeed.

This defect is a simulated training scenario created to demonstrate professional QA bug reporting.

---

## Preconditions

- The employee registration application is available.
- The employee registration form can be accessed.
- The application's age requirement is configured to accept ages 18 through 65, inclusive.

---

## Test Data

- **First Name:** Sergio
- **Email:** sergio@email.com
- **Age:** 65

---

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the employee registration form.
2. Enter "Sergio" in the First Name field.
3. Enter "sergio@email.com" in the Email field.
4. Enter "65" in the Age field.
5. Click the Register button.

---

## Expected Result

The application should successfully register the employee because age 65 meets the specified requirements.

A registration confirmation should appear, and an employee record should be created.

## Actual Result (Simulated)

The application rejects the registration and displays the hypothetical error:

"Employee must be younger than 65."

No employee record is created.

---

## Severity Assessment

**Proposed Severity: Medium**

The defect prevents a subset of otherwise eligible employees from registering.

Although it does not demonstrate a security or privacy vulnerability, it violates the application's age-validation requirements and affects core functionality for users aged exactly 65.

Additional testing would be required to determine whether other boundary values are affected.

---

## Priority Assessment

**Proposed Priority: Medium**

The issue prevents employees aged 65 from registering but appears limited to a specific boundary value.

Priority may need to increase if the feature is approaching release, affected users have no workaround, or the defect affects additional age values.

The final priority should be determined with the development and product teams.

---

## Suggested Follow-Up Tests

1. Verify that age 64 is accepted.
2. Verify that age 65 is accepted after the defect is fixed.
3. Verify that age 66 is rejected.
4. Verify that age 18 is accepted.
5. Verify that age 17 is rejected.
6. Investigate whether other out-of-range ages are incorrectly accepted.
